# Spare-point contact choices

An optional Explore contacts button is available during funded active construction once permits are granted, the power study has started and equipment has been ordered (or the connection is underway). It only appears when a contact visit is affordable and no live-work warning needs attention.

Opening the panel spends nothing. The existing choices show point costs, trust/score effects and character responses. This is a chance to use spare points while crews work, not a required next step or a construction-speed promise. Initial land, permit and power decisions stay first. Engine rules and visit costs are unchanged.
