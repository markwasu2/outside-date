# Graded site earth plane

The initial site dirt now has quiet, broken blade passes, faint paired tire impressions and an irregular cut-and-fill shoulder. The detail is part of the earth plane beneath the buildings. It is scenery, not a new road, purchased work or extra capacity.

An initial continuous loop and full-width stripes read like a sports pitch. Those were rejected after pixel review. The final version uses discontinuous low-contrast patches. All four terrain themes keep their own palette and surrounding fields, ledges or siding. Roads, pad coordinates, woodland and construction rules are unchanged.
