# Compact building captions

Simple campus captions keep the stage and progress without repeating "building" or "testing". For example, "Walls & roof: 83%" replaces "Walls & roof - building (83%)". The final-testing power blocker reads "Final testing needs power". The full original caption remains in its title. Foundation work is named "Foundation" rather than "Pad".
