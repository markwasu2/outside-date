# Lender funding-gap recovery

A six-seed real-click replay found one stalled campus: the lender's out-of-balance freeze was labeled paperwork, offered an ineffective Unfreeze action, and its error referred simple-mode players to a nonexistent Capital tab.

The simple view now distinguishes the funding-gap freeze from a paperwork freeze. Money displays the engine's own cost-to-complete, undrawn funding including pending finance, shortfall, and tolerance. It explains that bank cash is separate from the commitment test. Impossible paperwork cures are hidden until the financing gap clears. Work captions and the footer point to Money.

No engine rules or amounts changed. Seed 42's $4.9M shortfall exceeds the $1.7M tolerance. An equity call clears that commitment gap, while a separate paperwork freeze correctly remains. Clearing that paperwork resumes work. The campus opens and sells in month 15.

Verification: 707 unit tests, 178 browser tests in four shards, two built-app tests, lint and build. The recovery test also verifies the independent paperwork freeze remains after financing, clears after its own cure, and reaches the sale. Screenshots show the Money explanation, cleared work, and actual sale result.
