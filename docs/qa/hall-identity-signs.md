# Hall identity signs

Dark bare hall numbers were difficult to read over the night terrain. Simple-mode hall IDs now have light lettering on a green sign, joined visually to the status label below. Selected halls have a deeper green sign. Status labels have a solid pale backing, a fine border and a quiet shadow. Blocked status keeps its amber cue.

Detailed-mode labels, hall names, state wording, controls, selection and construction rules are unchanged. The sign ignores pointer input so clicking the label continues to select the hall.
