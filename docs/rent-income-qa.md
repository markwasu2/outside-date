# Rent income is not total cash profit

A real first-campus route opened the hall, reached a usual rent label of $628K/month, and showed a finished-month total cash change of -$34K. The old plus-prefixed ticker looked like a promise of net cash growth. Its amount is actually usual rent after the landlord share and far-pad line losses, before loan payments and other bills, ignoring temporary rent-free periods.

The ticker now says Rent instead of a bare plus amount. The open hall explains the difference, points to the actual monthly cash change and links directly to Money. An open hall is titled open for rent, not Build, and finished or terminated halls no longer show a meaningless repeat-build instruction. No engine calculation or cash amount changes.
